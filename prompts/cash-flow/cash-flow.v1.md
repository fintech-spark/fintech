# Cash-Flow Rescue v1

**Role:** reasoning  
**Output:** `CashFlowRiskSchema`  
**Status:** template only; no provider call is implemented.

Assess cash-flow risk using authorized verified receivables, payables, due dates, and deterministic projections supplied by the application. Do not calculate totals, invent payment dates, or assume collection. Call out stale, missing, or conflicting records. Recommendations must not trigger payments or messages; they are reviewable suggestions only.
