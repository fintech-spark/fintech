# Merchant Brain roadmap

This is a broad sequence only. It is not an architecture commitment and does not authorize implementation in this setup task.

1. **Project foundation** — requirements, design system, agent rules, checks, security, evaluation, and observability guardrails.
2. **Authentication and onboarding** — approved identity, merchant context, consent, and tenant boundaries.
3. **Data ingestion** — zero-entry paths for documents, messages, voice, CSV/Excel, and human review.
4. **Business intelligence** — verified business state, evidence, freshness, and conflict handling.
5. **Profit Leak Radar** — grounded leak detection and explanations.
6. **Cash Flow Rescue** — receivables/payables and cash-flow risk with verified calculations.
7. **What-If Simulator** — transparent, deterministic scenarios with explicit assumptions.
8. **AI Action Center** — draft/prepare/execute policy with confirmations and auditability.
9. **Business Assistant** — evidence-backed questions and explanations.
10. **Security, testing, evaluation, and polish** — hardening, accessibility, cost, reliability, and measured release readiness.

Each phase needs approved requirements, UI flows, data contracts, risk review, and a Definition of Done record before implementation begins.
